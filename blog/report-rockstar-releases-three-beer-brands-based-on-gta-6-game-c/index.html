Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Regulatory Discovery and Product Launch

According to reports from X, Rockstar is reportedly launching three new beer brands—Pißwasser, Patriot, and No Mames Vice City—produced by Tripping Animals Brewing. These beverages feature alcohol strengths of 4.2% ABV for Pißwasser and Patriot, and 6% ABV for No Mames Vice City, linking in-game references to real-world production.

Context and analysis

Uncertainties and Next Steps

While the report from X suggests Rockstar may be creating real products based on in-game alcohol references, several details remain unclear. There is no official confirmation from Rockstar or Take-Two regarding the authenticity of these claims, nor is there a public launch schedule. The TTB database discovery adds regulatory interest, but consumer availability depends on further verification and corporate statements.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
