Unconfirmed claims and evidence

What RockstarINTEL Reported

RockstarINTEL reported on Saturday that an employee at a retail store selling the album posted a video on Twitter showing the rear cover of the vinyl. According to the outlet, the cover lists all sides of the record plus the names and artists for 34 tracks. RockstarINTEL said fewer than a dozen of those tracks had been revealed publicly by Rockstar Games before the video appeared.

The outlet also highlighted fan reaction to the reported list. RockstarINTEL noted expectations that The Weeknd, who can be heard on in-game radio, would appear on the album, but said that is not the case. It also pointed to Lana Del Rey’s “Crazy for You,” described by RockstarINTEL as an unreleased 2014 song registered recently, and to Playboi Carti’s inclusion.

Context and analysis

What Remains Unconfirmed

Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive have not confirmed the track list, and RockstarINTEL framed the list as reportedly leaked. The report is based on a retail employee’s video, not an official announcement. The supplied excerpt did not reproduce the full 34-song list, so the complete sequencing and every entry remain unverified here.

RockstarINTEL said the album had been delayed by a day to November 20 and that the game was just days away at the time of publication. Readers should treat the track list as an unconfirmed report until Rockstar or Take-Two addresses it directly.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
