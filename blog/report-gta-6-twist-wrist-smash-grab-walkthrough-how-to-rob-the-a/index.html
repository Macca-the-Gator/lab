Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Mission Overview

According to VideogamesChronicle, the Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab mission in GTA 6 tasks players with teaming up with Billy and the Thrillbilly crew to intercept a Gruppe 6 armoured van and steal its cash.

VideogamesChronicle explains that after arriving at the meeting point, players must ride a dirt bike provided by Billy, follow him off‑road to the ambush location, stay close, and wait for the van to approach before using weapons to disable it.

Context and analysis

Execution Steps

VideogamesChronicle details that once the van reaches the designated spot, players should stay alongside on the dirt bike, fire at the vehicle until the crew forces it to a halt, causing it to lose control and crash.

After the crash, the guide instructs players to walk to the rear of the van, interact with the back doors to plant an explosive charge, move away before detonation, and collect the cash bags offered by Billy to complete the mission.
