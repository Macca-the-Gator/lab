Unconfirmed claims and evidence

What Game Rant Reported

Game Rant published a GTA 6 vehicle hub explaining that players can steal, purchase, and drive a wide variety of vehicles throughout Leonida. The guide includes helicopters and personal vehicles, and covers how to find, use, maintain, and drive the game's various vehicles.

The report organizes coverage into acquiring vehicles, vehicle management and maintenance, and driving mechanics. This information comes from Game Rant's guide coverage and does not include a Rockstar Games confirmation of the vehicle systems.

Context and analysis

What Remains Unknown

Game Rant's hub does not specify every vehicle model, exact purchase prices, or release details for GTA 6 itself. It also does not confirm which driving features are available at launch versus post-launch updates.

Readers should treat specific vehicle locations and mechanics as reported by Game Rant until Rockstar Games provides official details or an updated statement. The guide itself is a third-party report, not an official Rockstar announcement.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
