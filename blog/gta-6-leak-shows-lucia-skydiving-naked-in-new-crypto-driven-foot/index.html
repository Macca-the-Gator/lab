Leak Details

Kotaku reports that the anonymous account Cyberleek shared a five‑minute clip showing Lucia, the female lead of GTA 6, skydiving naked over Vice City while engaging in combat and visiting an ATM.

The leaker states the footage is being used to drive up the price of their $Cyberleek meme coin and promises longer, more explicit versions if the token’s market cap increases.

Context and Reactions

Cyberleek had previously threatened to release a playable build of GTA 6 if the crypto token reached a $30 million market cap; the coin currently sits around $4.75 million, according to Kotaku.

The outlet notes that this follows a prior episode where the leaker allegedly profited roughly $250 000 from a crypto pump‑and‑dump before going quiet for over a month.

What Remains Unknown

It is unclear whether the nude Lucia footage originates from an actual game build, a debug menu, or a modified version, and Rockstar/Take‑Two have not commented on the leak’s authenticity.

Whether the leaker will follow through on threats to release further content depends entirely on the volatile performance of the crypto token.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
