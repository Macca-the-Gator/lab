Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Console Pricing and Availability

GameSpot reports that players will need a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S to play GTA 6 at launch on November 19, 2026, with new console prices ranging from $500 for an Xbox Series S to $900 for a PlayStation 5 Pro.

The guide notes that certified refurbished units from Sony and Microsoft offer lower entry points at $500 for a PS5 and $400 for an Xbox Series S, while secondhand markets like eBay may provide further options.

Analyst Mat Piscatella of Circana is cited saying the game will sell phenomenally regardless of the lack of a physical disc edition.

Context and analysis

Game Editions and Digital-Only Release

The standard edition of GTA 6 is priced at $80 and the Ultimate Edition at $100, both digital-only with no disc version available; physical retail boxes will contain only a download code.

GameSpot explains that the Ultimate Edition includes exclusive cosmetics and in-game shops inaccessible to standard edition buyers, and preorders grant the Vintage Vice City Pack plus a free month of GTA+.

Rhys Elliott of Alinea Analytics is quoted suggesting the digital-exclusive strategy eliminates the secondhand market and maximizes revenue for Rockstar and Take-Two.

Additional context

Preloading, File Size, and Online Requirements

The game's file size is reported at 201GB, with preloading beginning November 12, a week before launch, though the game will not unlock until November 19.

GameSpot states that GTA 6 is a strictly single-player experience at launch, so PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass subscriptions are likely not required, though Rockstar has not confirmed this.

A new GTA Online component is expected in early 2027, at which point a subscription may become necessary.
