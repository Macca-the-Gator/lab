Avoiding Creative Distractions

In a recent interview with Variety, Rockstar Games co-founder Dan Houser revealed he has not watched Grand Theft Auto 6’s extended reveal, which premiered in August 2026 on Netflix. Houser, who left Rockstar in 2020 after co-writing and producing titles like GTA 3 through GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, stated that avoiding such content helps him steer clear of distractions that could disrupt his creative process. He emphasized that his team at Absurd Ventures now handles competitive research on other open-world games to prevent him from deconstructing their design choices.

New Projects at Absurd Ventures

Houser’s current studio, Absurd Ventures, is developing two new video games, including an open-world comedy and a dystopian sci-fi title centered on a rogue AI. While specifics remain under wraps, he hinted at upcoming reveals in the coming months. Additionally, Absurd Ventures has released audio dramas, comics, and novels as part of its multimedia approach. Houser reflected on his time at Rockstar, noting he never watched Western films or TV series like The Sopranos during development, preferring to isolate himself from external influences entirely.
