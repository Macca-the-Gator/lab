Halloween Event Week Continues

RockstarINTEL reports that the GTA Online Halloween Event Week runs from October 8 to 14, continuing the month-long Halloween event with new and returning spooky content.

Rewards and Bonuses

The Kortz Center Heist adds new Primary Targets, and completing it without dying awards an extra GTA$400,000. The FIB Priority File is The Black Box File, which pays double.

At the Casino, the lucky wheel prize is the Annis Savestra, and GTA+ members receive two spins per day. The Prize Ride vehicle is the Declasse Walton L35, unlocked by placing top 4 in LS Car Meet races for two consecutive days. The Premium Test Ride is the Maibatsu MonstroCiti for PS5, Xbox Series X/S and PC Enhanced. Test Track vehicles include the Declasse Vamos, Dewbauchee Seven-70 and Ocelot Locust. Luxury Autos shows the Dewbauchee Rapid GT X and Übermacht Sentinel XS4, while Simeon's Auto Shop offers the Albany Brigham, Albany Fränken Stange, Albany Lurcher, Declasse Tornado Rat Rod and LCC Sanctus.
