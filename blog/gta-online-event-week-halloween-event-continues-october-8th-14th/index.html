Halloween Event Week Continues

RockstarINTEL reports that GTA Online's Halloween Event Week runs from October 22 to 28, continuing the month-long celebration with a mix of new and returning spooky content. Players can expect themed missions, rewards, and bonuses throughout the week, as Rockstar rotates various in-game activities to keep the event fresh.

Heist & Missions Bonuses

According to the publisher, the Kortz Center Heist will receive new Primary Targets, its first-completion bonus will reset, and completing the heist without dying will award an extra GTA$400,000. Additionally, the FIB Priority File for this week is The Fine Art File, which pays double payouts for all participants.

Vehicles & In-Game Deals

This week's Casino spin prize is the Pfister Comet S2, valued at $1,797,000, with GTA+ members receiving two spins per 24 hours. The Prize Ride vehicle is the Grotti Itali Classic, unlockable by placing top 2 in LS Car Meet races for two consecutive days. The Premium Test Ride is the Maibatsu MonstroCiti for enhanced platforms, while the Test Track features the Declasse Vamos, Dewbauchee Seven-70, and Ocelot Locust. Luxury Autos is showcasing the Dewbauchee Rapid GT X and Übermacht Sentinel XS4, and Simeon's Auto Shop offers the Albany Brigham, Albany Fränken Stange, Albany Lurcher, Declasse Tornado Rat Rod, and LCC Sanctus.
