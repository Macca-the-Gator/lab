Event Schedule and Dates

RockstarINTEL announces that the Halloween Event Week in GTA Online will run from October 15 through October 21, 2026, extending the ongoing month‑long Halloween celebration introduced earlier in the month.

During this week, players can expect new and returning spooky missions, updated rewards, and rotating content across the game world, as detailed by the RockstarINTEL report.

Rewards and Bonuses

Completing the Kortz Center Heist without dying during this event week grants an additional GTA$400,000, resetting the first‑completion bonus and providing a substantial payout for skilled players, according to RockstarINTEL.

The FIB Priority File this week is The Brute Force File, which awards double payouts, and the Casino’s lucky wheel offers GTA+ members two daily spins for a chance to win the Grotti LSCM Cheetah Classic.

Additional Content and Vehicles

This week’s GTA Online Premium Test Ride vehicle is the Maibatsu MonstroCiti, available for PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC Enhanced players, while the Test Track features the Declasse Vamos, Dewbauchee Seven‑70, and Ocelot Locust, as reported by RockstarINTEL.

Car dealerships in Rockford Hills showcase the Dewbauchee Rapid GT X and Übermacht Sentinel XS4, and Simeon’s Auto Shop offers five vehicles for test drives, including the Albany Brigham, Albany Fränken Stange, Albany Lurcher, Declasse Tornado Rat Rod, and LCC Sanctus, expanding player options during the event.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
