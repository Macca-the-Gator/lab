Confirmed facts

Free Vehicle and Paint Perks

GTA+ members this month receive the exclusive Grotti Itali RSX, a luxury sports car normally priced at $3,465,000, which is available for a week at no cost. The vehicle can be claimed by visiting The Vinewood Car Club, where it appears as a FREE option for subscribers only. In addition, two new Chameleon paints – the White Prismatic Pearl Chamelon Paint for both car and wheels – are also free. These color finishes can be applied at any Auto Shop or Vehicle Workshop by selecting the eligible vehicle and choosing the Chameleon Color Group, which is marked FREE for GTA+ members.

Context and analysis

Additional Storage and Discounts

Rockstar added extra storage benefits for GTA+ users. The McKenzie Field Hangar’s recent expansion already gives 15 additional aircraft slots, and this month’s GTA+ bonus grants five more slots, usable even without the hangar. The exclusive Vinewood Car Club location, only accessible to paying members, features a rotating roster of test‑drive vehicles sold at a guaranteed 20 % discount. Subscribers also gain access to the Vinewood Club Garage in Pillbox Hill, which holds up to 100 vehicles. While active, GTA+ members keep permanent access to all garage cars; storage pauses if the subscription lapses.

Additional context

Claim Window and Subscription Details

The November GTA+ cycle runs from November 5 through December 2, giving members a four‑week window to secure all new perks. A standard subscription costs $7.99/£6.99/€7.99. The Grotti Itali RSX, Chameleon paints, extra hangar slots, and Vinewood Car Club discounts are automatically added once members log into the Vinewood Car Club and an Auto Shop. Once the subscription ends, access to the garage and any stored vehicles is suspended until the service is renewed.
