Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Mission Overview and Structure

VideoGamesChronicle reports that Dubious Provenance is a three-part mission unlocked after completing the Chapter 3 mission Dealer's Choice. The guide notes that players must wait for an in-game prompt or a call from Halifax before the next stage becomes available. The mission begins at the Bayside apartment where Jason and Lucia interact with a bag in the closet, then travel to Evelyn Andrews Antiques in Capri to have a mysterious statue appraised. Evelyn refuses, but her associate Benedict Halifax offers a private appraisal in exchange for his Crowex Octaine "Bobby Ambrose" watch as collateral.

After handing over the statue, Halifax disappears without providing an appraisal. Players must then wait for time to pass before returning to the antiques store to break in via the back roof and a window above an air conditioning unit. VGC's walkthrough provides step-by-step navigation for each phase, including the requirement to let in-game time advance between stages.

Context and analysis

The Halifax Decision and Consequences

The guide highlights the pivotal choice during the mission's final section: after chasing Halifax across Capri in a golf cart, players must shoot him in the leg to stop his escape and then decide whether to kill him or let him go. VGC recommends sparing Halifax, noting that shooting him ends his involvement violently while sparing him allows Jason and Lucia to loot the lockup they tracked him to. The article states that either choice allows completion of Dubious Provenance and progression in the story, but sparing Halifax avoids an unnecessary execution.

VGC's companion piece on the same decision elaborates that sparing Halifax yields the same mission completion and rewards, with the only difference being the narrative outcome. The guide emphasizes that the choice is ultimately player preference, but provides clear information on what each path entails so players can decide based on their desired story outcome.

Additional context

Rewards and Mission Completion

According to VGC, completing Dubious Provenance grants players the Crowex Octaine watch as a collectible item and advances the storyline involving the mysterious statue. The guide does not specify additional monetary or experience rewards beyond the narrative progression. The walkthrough also links to VGC's complete GTA 6 hub for beginner tips, navigation explanations, and cheats, positioning this mission guide as part of a broader resource for players.
