Confirmed facts

Kick’s Sunset Reward Case Details

Kick’s Sunset Reward Case runs from November 19 to December 7, offering 10,000 digital GTA VI Ultimate Edition codes for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with PS5 codes restricted to supported PlayStation Network regions while Xbox codes are worldwide.

Viewers must watch any Kick stream for three consecutive hours to unlock a daily reward case, then open the case next to their avatar for a chance to win a game code, with a new case released each day and an extra free case for new users after onboarding.

Context and analysis

Twitch Daily Code Drops and GTA Rewards

During TwitchCon San Diego 2026, Twitch announced daily GTA VI code drops from launch until the end of November, though the total number of codes and specific entry requirements remain undisclosed.

Twitch is also adding GTA-themed Sub Emotes, Badges, an evolving viewer badge for GTA stream watchers, and exclusive Heist Hype Train emotes for participating streams, enhancing the community experience.
