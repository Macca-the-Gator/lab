Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Glitch Unlocks Higher Frame Rates on Xbox Series X|S

RockstarINTEL reports that a player-discovered glitch allows Grand Theft Auto VI to exceed its 30FPS console cap on Xbox Series X and Series S.

The exploit relies on the console’s Quick Resume feature and requires a 120Hz display, according to the outlet.

Instead of a stable 60FPS, the glitch unlocks the frame rate entirely, typically yielding performance in the 40FPS range with occasional cutscenes reaching 100FPS.

Context and analysis

Gameplay Physics Run at Double Speed

The publication notes a significant caveat: because the game’s physics are tied to frame rate, unlocking it causes gameplay animations to run at twice the intended speed.

Cutscenes remain unaffected, but the accelerated physics may appeal to speedrunners while detracting from normal play.

RockstarINTEL also warns that the frame rate is unstable and that variable refresh rate (VRR) can mitigate but not eliminate the issue.

Additional context

Discovery Credited to Reddit User; Patch Likely

The glitch was first spotted by Reddit user u/Meowwie64 and is exclusive to Xbox due to its dependence on Quick Resume.

RockstarINTEL speculates that Rockstar Games may patch the exploit or request Microsoft to block it on the platform level.

Players interested in testing the method are advised to do so soon, as the workaround may not persist in future updates.
