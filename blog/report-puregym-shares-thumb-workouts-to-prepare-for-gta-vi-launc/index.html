PureGym Thumb Workouts for GTA VI

GameSpot reports that fitness company PureGym shared three thumb workouts from personal trainer David Hope to help players prepare for Grand Theft Auto VI. The routine includes tapping the thumb with each finger on the same hand, forming a fist with the thumb on the outside, and performing finger pushups by pressing both hands fingertips into the floor. PureGym recommends repeating each exercise 10 times before and after extended gaming sessions.

Context and analysis

GTA VI Release Date and Related Reports

According to GameSpot, Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled to release on November 19. The outlet also cited an economics professor projecting that 1.5 million people in America could call in sick around the launch, and noted that Miami's Kaseya Center, home of the NBA's Heat, currently features GTA-themed Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell food locations.

Additional context

What Remains Unknown

GameSpot's report does not state whether Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive endorsed the PureGym routine, and it provides no methodology for the economics professor's projection. Details on how long the Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell offerings will remain at Kaseya Center were also not included.
