Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Xbox OS-Level VRR Claim

RockstarINTEL reports, citing Digital Foundry's analysis, that Grand Theft Auto VI runs inside a 120Hz Variable Refresh Rate container on Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S at the Xbox OS level, regardless of the game's frame rate, so developers do not need to implement VRR themselves. The publication says this can make the 30FPS-capped game feel smoother on VRR-capable displays, and notes players can enable the setting in Xbox's display menu if their TV or monitor supports it.

Context and analysis

PS5 VRR Limit and Frame-Rate Notes

The same report states that PlayStation 5's OS-level VRR only works when a game runs above 48FPS, and because GTA VI targets 30FPS, PS5 players cannot use it. RockstarINTEL adds that the game has dropped to 22FPS in demanding areas, and argues the PS5 Pro delivers the most stable frame rate and a sharper image via PSSR, though at a higher cost.

Additional context

What Remains Unverified

These findings are third-party technical reporting, not an official Rockstar Games or Take-Two announcement, and Rockstar has not publicly confirmed them. Precise frame-time measurements, how the Xbox container behaves across every display, and the extent of the PS5 Pro advantage remain unverified, so players should treat the performance claims as reported, not official.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
