Rockstar’s Development Timeline Claim

According to a Bloomberg report by Jason Schreier, senior Rockstar staff said that the technology, tools and team built during GTA 6 will enable them to develop the next game more quickly, avoiding the eight‑year development cycle of GTA VI.

What Remains Unclear

Rockstar has not announced a specific release window for its next title, and the exact timeline has not been confirmed; the claim is based on an interview and not an official statement.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
