What the report says

Dexerto reports that GTA 6's 'advanced' weather system has finally been confirmed after years of rumors. The article's headline presents the confirmation as a new development, but the available RSS excerpt does not include any technical details, footage, or an official Rockstar quote.

As with any single-source report, this should be treated as a claim from Dexerto rather than independently confirmed information.

What remains unknown

At the moment, it is unclear what makes the weather system 'advanced,' how it will work in gameplay, or whether it will be shown before launch. The excerpt also does not say whether Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive made a formal announcement or if the confirmation came through another channel.

Context

Weather has long been a rumored focus for GTA 6, with fans speculating about dynamic storms and environmental effects. Until Rockstar or Take-Two shares more, the scope of the system remains a report rather than a verified feature. Anyone looking for official confirmation will need to wait for Rockstar to release further details.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
