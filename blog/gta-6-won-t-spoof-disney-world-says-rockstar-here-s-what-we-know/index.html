No Disney World Spoof in GTA 6

According to GameSpot, GTA 6 will not feature its own take on Walt Disney World or other Orlando-area attractions. The report cites a Game Informer interview with Rockstar's Jack McPherson, who confirmed that the game won't include those specific locations, stressing that the team instead focused on delivering the 'full Florida experience' through a curated mix of landscapes and landmarks.

What’s Excluded – And What’s Still Possible

GameSpot adds that SeaWorld and the Space Coast (including Cape Canaveral) are also absent. The publication notes this isn't surprising given Vice City's Miami setting, which is about 230 miles from Orlando. However, Rockstar could still reference the park via radio chatter or other nods, and a previous GTA 5 actor mused about a hypothetical 'Psycho Park.' Fans will have to wait to see if any Easter eggs surface.

The report also reminds us that GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. As with all pre-release details, treat this as a report from GameSpot and Game Informer – not an official full map reveal.
