A Grand Theft Auto Vision of London

"What if London existed in the Grand Theft Auto universe?" This question has been answered by YouTuber CityEd, who has crafted a "GTA-ised" map of the city, complete with reimagined place names in the classic Grand Theft Auto style, as reported by Eurogamer.net. CityEd's project transforms iconic London locations, for instance, turning Kings Cross into 'Dukes Cross' and Angel into 'Saint', while the entire city is dubbed 'Trafalgar'. This imaginative endeavor offers a unique look at how the English capital might appear within Rockstar Games' satirical world.

Applying Rockstar's Signature Style

CityEd's thought experiment meticulously applied Rockstar Games' naming conventions, which involve finding historical or cultural associations, exaggerating them, and using them as fictional place names. The fan cartographer referenced past GTA titles to incorporate the studio's signature satire, exaggerated landmarks, and distinct cartography style. Unlike the 1999 GTA: London mission pack, which did not rename the city in a similar fashion, CityEd's map started from scratch. A 30-minute video further details CityEd's creative process, explaining how real-world history influenced the map's design, and a PDF copy is available on their Patreon, Eurogamer.net notes.

Broader Grand Theft Auto Developments

The anticipation for the next official installment, Grand Theft Auto 6, continues to build, with its release expected in under two months, according to Eurogamer.net. The upcoming map is reportedly vast, capable of accommodating at least two Grand Theft Auto 5 maps. In related news, Rockstar Games has also revealed a £350 box of GTA 6 collectibles. Furthermore, weeks before the game's launch, Rockstar Games has implemented new guidelines that restrict mods for missions, maps, updated storylines, and third-party intellectual property, as also reported by Eurogamer.net.
