Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Leaker Ceases Activity Amid Meme Coin Collapse

According to RockstarINTEL, the leaker known as Cyberleek has ended their campaign of GTA 6 leaks following the failure of their meme coin venture. The leaker previously claimed access to extensive gameplay footage and snippets of the game’s ending, though RockstarINTEL notes the latest leak contained only out-of-context clips from final missions, reportedly revealing no critical spoilers.

Cyberleek’s departure comes after selling tokens tied to their project, which they framed as donations rather than investments. RockstarINTEL highlights the leaker’s cryptic statements about a 'dead man’s switch' that could activate if they are apprehended, potentially exposing additional story details. However, the leaker insists the ending will remain unpublished.

Context and analysis

Fan Concerns and Community Response

While RockstarINTEL emphasizes that Cyberleek’s claims about a dead man’s switch remain unverified, fans have expressed mixed reactions. Some speculate the leaker’s threats are performative, given their history of dramatic announcements. Others remain cautious, particularly after the leaker mentioned a $30 million milestone that was allegedly unmet, preventing the release of a full game build.

The leaker’s meme coin crash has also sparked criticism from the community, with some accusing them of using the GTA 6 leaks as a marketing ploy. RockstarINTEL’s article avoids detailing specific spoilers, instead serving as a warning for those hoping to avoid story revelations. As of now, no official statement from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive has been issued regarding these developments.
