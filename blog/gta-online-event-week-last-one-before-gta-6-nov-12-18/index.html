Confirmed facts

Kortz Center Heist and Priority File Rewards

RockstarINTEL reported that the GTA Online event week running November 12 through November 18 is the last weekly update before GTA 6. The publisher said Rockstar has rotated content and added bonuses, including new Primary Targets for the Kortz Center Heist.

According to RockstarINTEL, the Kortz Center Heist's first-completion bonus will reset, and completing the heist without dying awards an extra GTA$400,000. The FIB Priority File for the week is The Brute Force File, and Priority Files pay double during the event.

Context and analysis

Cars, Casino Prizes and Test Rides

RockstarINTEL also listed the week's reward vehicles and dealership offers. Players can try the Weeny Issi Rally as the Premium Test Ride, while the Test Track includes the Übermacht Rebla GTS, Declasse Impaler LX and Obey I-Wagen.

The Lucky Wheel casino prize this week is the Karin Everon, listed by RockstarINTEL at $1,475,000 from Southern San Andreas Super Autos, with GTA+ members receiving two spins per 24 hours. LS Car Meet players who place top four in a race for two days in a row can unlock the Western Powersurge.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
