Confirmed facts

Report Details

Kotaku's report confirms that shrink-wrapped Grand Theft Auto 6 PlayStation 5 boxes have started appearing in retail locations ahead of the November 12 street date, with the packaging containing no physical disc and requiring an internet connection to redeem the included digital download code. The images originated from a German retail worker's post on the GTA6unmoderrated subreddit, which was later deleted, reflecting the high-alert environment surrounding the game's imminent November 19 launch.

Context and analysis

Disc-Less Context

The article explains that Take-Two's disc-free strategy for GTA 6 serves as an early test of Sony's planned PlayStation 5 disc-less future beginning in 2028, while also noting that some physical boxes may contain redemption codes to preserve retail shelf space. Additionally, Kotaku mentions that pre-loading for the title opens on November 10, allowing players to take home empty display cases a week before the November 19 online launch, and that collector interest in empty boxes is already evident among fans.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
