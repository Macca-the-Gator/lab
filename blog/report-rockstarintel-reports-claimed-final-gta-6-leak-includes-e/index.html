RockstarINTEL Report on Claimed GTA 6 Ending Leak

RockstarINTEL reports that what appears to be the final Grand Theft Auto VI gameplay leak from the account CYBERLEEK has been released, including a snippet from the game's ending. The outlet says it is examining what the leaker said and will not publish specific ending spoilers. The report is attributed to RockstarINTEL, not Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

Leaker's Alleged Statements and Dead Man's Switch

According to RockstarINTEL, the leaker claims the memecoin tied to him collapsed and prompted his decision to quit. The outlet also quotes the leaker as saying he possesses the ending but chose not to publish it to protect players, and that a dead man's switch could release story spoilers if he is caught. These are unverified claims relayed by RockstarINTEL.

Ending Spoiler Warning

This coverage involves potential Grand Theft Auto VI ending spoilers. RockstarINTEL states the leak contains out-of-context clips from final missions but avoids printing plot details. Readers seeking to remain spoiler-free should be cautious around related clips and discussions. No official Rockstar or Take-Two confirmation is included in the report.

Sources and notes
