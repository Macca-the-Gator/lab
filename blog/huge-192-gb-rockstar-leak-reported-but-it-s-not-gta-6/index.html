What the Report Says

According to a report from Notebookcheck, a massive 192 GB leak from Rockstar Games has surfaced, arriving roughly two months before the expected launch of GTA 6. The report states that the leaked data "has little to do with GTA 6," suggesting it may involve other Rockstar projects or older titles, though no specifics are provided.

What Remains Unknown

The report does not detail the exact contents of the leak, the platform or method by which it was obtained, or how it might impact Rockstar or Take-Two. As of now, these details are unverified and should be treated as a preliminary claim. Further corroboration or official statements will be needed to clarify the nature and significance of the leak.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
