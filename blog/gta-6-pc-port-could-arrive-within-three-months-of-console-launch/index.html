Report Details

GameSpot, citing a GamesBeat report, says industry sources claim Rockstar Games plans to launch Grand Theft Auto 6 on PC within the first three months of 2027. The game is expected to debut on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in November 2026.

The sources described the PC release as occurring just a few months after the console launch, which would be a departure from previous Rockstar titles that often waited over a year before arriving on PC.

Context and Unknowns

Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick previously noted that PC is becoming a more important platform for the company, and Rockstar has not officially confirmed a PC version of GTA 6.

It remains unclear whether the reported timeline is final, and no official announcement has been made by Rockstar or Take-Two.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
