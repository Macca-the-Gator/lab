Confirmed facts

Release Timeline and Box Copy Distribution

According to the RockstarINTEL report, physical copies of GTA 6 have started arriving at major retailers on November 12, with retailers expected to list the discs for sale beginning November 19, the official launch date.

Take-Two and Rockstar confirmed that the game cases were shipped to stores and some staff posted images showing the back cover, which lists a 230GB installation requirement and an online functions icon.

Context and analysis

New Screenshots and Online Feature Insights

The article includes three newly released screenshots depicting the protagonists Lucia and Jason alongside a plane flying over the Keys, providing the first real‑world visual glimpse of the game's characters and environment.

An icon indicating online functions on the case suggests either a forthcoming update to GTA Online next year or the ability to purchase the Ultimate Edition upgrade directly from within the game, though Rockstar has not clarified the exact purpose.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
