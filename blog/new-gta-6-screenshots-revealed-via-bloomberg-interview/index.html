Official Screenshots Released

RockstarINTEL reports that Bloomberg published two new official GTA 6 screenshots after granting Jason Schreier access to Rockstar North for an interview with senior staff.

What the Screenshots Show

One image captures Lucia and Jason Schreier covered in mud while drinking beer in an off‑road/dirt racing area of Leonida. The other shows a rear view of a car on Ocean Drive at sunset, identified as a new Pegassi model dubbed the GranForma, with a police helicopter flying low.

Additional Context and Unknowns

The screenshots accompany a Bloomberg Businessweek cover story and are paired with illustrations by Viktor Hachmang. While the images confirm certain visual details, Rockstar has not disclosed a release date or further gameplay specifics.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
