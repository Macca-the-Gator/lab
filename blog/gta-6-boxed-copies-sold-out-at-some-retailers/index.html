Retail Sell‑out Confirmed

Amazon US and Target have listed GTA 6’s physical "code‑in‑a‑box" edition as sold out, according to a report from RockstarINTEL. The listings show "Currently unavailable" for the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions as of October 11, while several Target stores in major US cities have "Preorders sold out" notices.

Rockstar has indicated that the physical edition is produced in limited quantities, with the company’s website stating "while supplies last." This scarcity has driven rapid sell‑through as the November 12 launch approaches.

Impact on Fans and Collectors

For collectors, the limited run makes the boxed edition especially desirable, often priced below the digital version. Amazon UK, for example, offers the game at £65, £5 under the standard digital price, giving buyers a tangible item without a premium cost.

However, the low inventory also means many fans risk missing out on the physical release. Those who pre‑ordered earlier may receive refunds or delayed shipments, prompting discussions about the value of immediate digital access versus a physical collectible.

Rockstar’s Limited Quantities Policy

Rockstar confirmed back in June that only limited numbers of the physical GTA 6 edition would be produced. The "while supplies last" policy, combined with retailer sell‑outs, underscores a strategy that balances collector demand with controlled distribution. The upcoming weeks will reveal whether additional stock arrives or the scarcity continues.
