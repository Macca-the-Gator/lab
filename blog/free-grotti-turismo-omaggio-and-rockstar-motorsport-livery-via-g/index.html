Confirmed facts

Twitch Drops Reward Details

RockstarINTEL reported that Rockstar Games and Twitch have partnered to bring back Twitch Drops for GTA V, offering players a free Grotti Turismo Omaggio supercar and the Rockstar Motorsport Livery. The outlet said the car is worth $2,845,000 at Legendary Motorsport and that the livery previously appeared on the Pegassi Zentorno through GTA+ earlier this year.

According to RockstarINTEL, the Drops run from October 30 through November 1, 2026, and players must watch select NoPixel V roleplay streamers on Twitch for four hours to earn the rewards. The streamers will display the NoPixel V server logo in their names, and only streams with Drops Enabled count. Rewards must be claimed by Monday, November 2 at 11:59 BST, and players must have their Rockstar Games account connected to Twitch.

Context and analysis

Eligibility Steps and Unanswered Details

RockstarINTEL noted that the rewards will be usable in the vanilla version of GTA Online on all consoles and PC. The report did not specify the full list of participating streamers, whether the car and livery are permanent unlocks, or if any additional in-game requirements apply beyond the watch time. Rockstar Games has not issued a separate announcement in the coverage cited by RockstarINTEL.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
