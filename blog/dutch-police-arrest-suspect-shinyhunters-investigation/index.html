Police Confirm an Arrest in the ShinyHunters Investigation

Dutch police say a 24-year-old man from Amsterdam was arrested on September 15 on suspicion of involvement with the ShinyHunters cybercrime group. The Rotterdam court has ordered him held for another 90 days while the investigation continues. An arrest is not a conviction, and police say further arrests remain possible.

Police also say information about two planned killings was found on the suspect?s laptop, and that he is suspected of attempted incitement to murder. Authorities explicitly state that this separate allegation is not part of the ShinyHunters investigation. The Associated Press has independently reported the arrest and the police account.

What the Reporting Says About Rockstar Games

RockstarINTEL connects the arrest to the group?s earlier claimed breach of Rockstar Games. Dutch police and the Associated Press confirm an investigation into the suspect?s alleged ShinyHunters role, but do not say that he personally took part in the Rockstar breach. That distinction remains important while both investigations continue.

The arrest adds a new development to coverage of the group, while leaving the suspect?s individual role in the earlier Rockstar incident unconfirmed by the authorities.
