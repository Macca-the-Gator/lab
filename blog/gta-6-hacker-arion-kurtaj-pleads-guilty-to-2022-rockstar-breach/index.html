Confirmed facts

Guilty Plea at Woolwich Crown Court

On November 16, 2026, Arion Kurtaj, now 21, pleaded guilty at Woolwich Crown Court in London to all charges related to the 2022 breach of Rockstar Games’ servers, according to BBC reporting cited by RockstarINTEL, PC Gamer, Kotaku, and Rock Paper Shotgun. Kurtaj, a member of the hacking group Lapsus$, admitted to stealing and releasing nearly 100 videos of unfinished Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay, as well as other cybercrimes including fraud and physical assaults.

Context and analysis

Background and Legal Proceedings

Kurtaj carried out the intrusion while on bail for previous offenses and staying at a Travelodge hotel under police protection, using an Amazon Fire Stick, a Bluetooth keyboard, and a mouse to access Rockstar’s internal Slack and threaten the company, per Rock Paper Shotgun. He was initially deemed unfit to stand trial due to acute autism and sentenced to indefinite detention in a secure hospital, but a January 2026 review found him fit to proceed, leading to today’s guilty plea, Kotaku reports. Sentencing is scheduled for a later date.

Additional context

Impact and Ongoing Leaks

Rockstar stated the breach cost the company $5 million in recovery and thousands of staff hours, according to Rock Paper Shotgun. The 2022 leak was followed by another wave of GTA 6 footage in 2024 from a source known as “Cyberleek,” prompting further legal action by Take-Two. Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to release on November 19, 2026, with a mandatory pre-load update already issued, RockstarINTEL notes. A separate hacker arrest in September involving violent crimes was also reported.
