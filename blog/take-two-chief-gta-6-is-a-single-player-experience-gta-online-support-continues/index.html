Reported Statements

A Reddit post, aggregated via Google News, quotes a Take-Two chief as saying that GTA 6 is a 'single-player experience' and that the company will continue to support GTA Online. The post does not provide direct quotes or a specific executive name, and the original source appears to be unverified.

What Remains Unknown

It is unclear whether these comments were made during an investor call, interview, or other public appearance. No official Rockstar Games or Take-Two press release has confirmed this information. As with any unverified report, readers should treat this as a rumor until an official announcement is made.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
