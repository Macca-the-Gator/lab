Rahul Kohli's Multi-Era Spinoff Pitch

British actor Rahul Kohli, known for roles in 'Saros' and 'Midnight Mass', has shared an enthusiastic pitch for a new L.A. Noire spinoff. Speaking to BAFTA Games, Kohli proposed a game where players act as a cop across different historical periods. He suggested a Victorian England setting investigating Jack the Ripper, as well as a 1980s California setting pursuing the Night Stalker. Kohli expressed deep admiration for Team Bondi's original facial-capture technology, stating he wants a new game to use the exact same engine and tech without modern improvements to preserve the original charm.

Context and analysis

Franchise Future and Unconfirmed Rumors

While Kohli's pitch is purely a fan concept, the L.A. Noire franchise is reportedly being explored for future projects. Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick previously stated that the company is looking at future projects across all its franchises, including L.A. Noire. Additionally, a recent unconfirmed rumor suggests a new L.A. Noire game is in development, focusing on players collecting clues to track down the Zodiac Killer. Rockstar has not officially announced any new titles in the series since the 2017 VR release.
