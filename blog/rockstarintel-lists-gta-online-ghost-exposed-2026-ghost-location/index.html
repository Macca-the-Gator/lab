GTA Online Ghost Exposed returns

RockstarINTEL reports that Rockstar Games brought back the GTA Online Ghost Exposed collectibles for Halloween, starting with the current event week. The post says players receive an in-game email titled 'Ghost Exposed' and are asked to submit Snapmatic photos of ghosts in Blaine County.

Locations and rewards

According to RockstarINTEL, the 2026 ghost locations differ from last year and the final ghost is a different GTA V character from previous years. The guide credits GTA Series for the locations and a one-second lobby cooldown, and says ghosts appear in a fixed order between 8 p.m. and 6 a.m. in-game time. Each ghost is visible for about two real-world minutes at one of two positions, and the tenth ghost appears only after the first nine photos are completed.

The post reports each successful photo grants $20,000 GTA Online cash and 500 RP, with rewards doubled from October 1 through October 7. Completing all 10 photos reportedly gives a $50,000 bonus and the Ghosts Exposed Jacket. It does not confirm an exact end date, though it suggests the event will not last much beyond October 31.

Sources and notes
