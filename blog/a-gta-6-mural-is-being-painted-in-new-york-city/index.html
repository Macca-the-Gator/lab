Mural Project Overview

Rockstar Games is actively painting an official promotional mural for Grand Theft Auto VI in New York City. The artwork continues Rockstar's longstanding tradition of decorating large buildings with GTA-themed imagery to build hype ahead of the release.

Location and Design Details

The mural is situated at the intersection of Wythe Avenue and North 11th Street in Williamsburg, Brooklyn. It prominently features the main characters Jason and Lucia from previous GTA titles, with the iconic GTA logo integrated into the design. The piece is still in its early stages and is slated for completion within the coming days.
