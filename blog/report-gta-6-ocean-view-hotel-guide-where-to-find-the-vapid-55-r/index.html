Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Where the Ocean View Hotel sits in GTA 6

Video Games Chronicle reports that the Ocean View Hotel, a returning landmark from GTA: Vice City, can be found on Vice City's waterfront, mirroring its real-life counterpart, The Clevelander Hotel in Miami. VGC says you cannot miss the building, pointing to the huge glowing signs at the front of the hotel and the procession of supercars regularly parked outside it.

The same VGC guide states that this part of the map becomes available as soon as players get free access to the open world at the beginning of Chapter 1, so the hotel is not locked behind late-game progress.

Context and analysis

What the hotel gives you: the '55 Rapid Stainer

According to VGC, the main reason to visit is to pick up one of the cars included in the Grand Theft Auto 6 Ultimate Edition: the '55 Rapid Stainer, which the publication says can be found outside The Ocean View Hotel as part of the game's Vintage Vice City pack.

VGC notes the placement is a deliberate reference, echoing both a similar car parked outside the real hotel in Miami and the car that sat outside The Ocean View in GTA: Vice City. Note: this section contains a spoiler for a vehicle collectible location.

Additional context

What is confirmed and what is not

These details come from a third-party guide published by Video Games Chronicle, not from a Rockstar Games or Take-Two announcement, so they should be treated as reported guide information rather than official confirmation.

VGC has not published a map coordinate breakdown, screenshot-by-screenshot walkthrough or further Vintage Vice City pack item locations in the summary available, so it remains unclear whether other bonuses in the pack are tied to specific Vice City landmarks.
