Development Milestones

Rob Nelson of Rockstar North explains GTA 6 is built through a series of eight-week milestones where selected game pieces are compiled, evaluated, and refined iteratively.

The process involves creating assets and systems first, then roughing in content across all development areas, course-correcting year after year toward the finished vision.

NPC and World Detail

GTA 6 writing director James Horton emphasizes no shortcuts when building a vast world, focusing on making every element ring true from distant conversations to small room details.

Rockstar launched a dedicated LA team for pedestrian dialogue and NPC creation, while developers visited Florida strip clubs and rode along with police to authenticate Vice City details.
