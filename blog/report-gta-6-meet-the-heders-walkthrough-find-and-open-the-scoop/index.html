Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Mission Overview and Dinner Sequence

According to VideoGamesChronicle, the Meet the Heders mission begins after Dealer's Choice, with players switching to Lucia at the apartment where Cal discusses the mysterious hard drive. Brian Heder then contacts the player to arrange a dinner at a rooftop restaurant on Vice City's eastern side. VGC notes that choosing the Surf N Turf option when ordering provides a small relationship bonus. After dinner, Jason follows Brian to the balcony for a cigar conversation before the perspective shifts to Lucia for the vehicle theft portion.

Context and analysis

Locating and Opening the SCOOP Container

VGC's guide instructs players to drive Lucia across the bridge to a nearby car park, locate a white van, and change into a disguise before driving to the docks entrance. Once inside, players must search for yellow shipping containers marked SCOOP. The guide specifies that the required container sits on top of the middle stack, and while other SCOOP containers can be broken into, they do not advance the mission. The walkthrough does not detail the exact method for opening the container, only its location.
