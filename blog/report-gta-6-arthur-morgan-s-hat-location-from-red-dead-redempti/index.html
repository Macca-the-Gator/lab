How to Obtain Arthur Morgan’s Hat in GTA 6

VideoGamesChronicle reports that Arthur Morgan’s iconic hat from Red Dead Redemption 2 appears in Grand Theft Auto 6 as a wearable cosmetic item called the Genuine Cowboy Hat, which can be purchased for $35 at a specific yard sale location.

Context and analysis

Location, Requirements, and Character Restrictions

The yard sale is located on the southern edge of Port Gellhorn, a short distance south of Moses’ chop shop, and becomes accessible during daytime from Chapter 2 onward; each protagonist can buy the hat only once, so players must choose whether Jason or Lucia will wear it.

Additional context

Additional Coverage and Unconfirmed Details

GamesRadar published a similar guide on the same day corroborating the location and price, though neither outlet confirms whether the hat is a brand‑new replica or an aged original, and Rockstar Games has not officially commented on the crossover Easter egg.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
