Confirmed facts

Extended Trailer Airing on TV

RockstarINTEL reports that an extended cut of the GTA 6 launch trailer began airing on television during the NBA game between the Philadelphia 76ers and the New York Knicks on NBC. The trailer was shown in a commercial break, and a fan recorded the broadcast, noting several differences from the online version.

Context and analysis

Key Differences From the Online Cut

The TV version includes a longer clip of Raul Bautista dancing after the first shower scene, extended footage of Lucia jumping onto the plane landing gear, and a brief extension of Jason nudging Lucia. The online cut omits the shower kiss and Lucia's ankle tag, while the TV edition adds a longer sequence of Jason attempting to retrieve a knife and a slightly extended dance from Roxy. A separate 60‑second spot also circulates, trimming several opening scenes.
