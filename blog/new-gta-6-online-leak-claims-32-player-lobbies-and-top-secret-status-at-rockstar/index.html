What the Leak Claims

EGW.News has published a new report claiming that GTA 6 Online will use 32-player lobbies, a departure from GTA Online's current setup. The outlet also says the project is being considered 'top secret' at Rockstar Games, though it provides no direct evidence.

As with any leak, these details are unverified and should be treated as rumor until Rockstar or Take-Two officially comment.

What Remains Unknown

EGW.News does not clarify whether the 32-player lobbies would apply to all modes or only certain activities, nor does it give a timeframe for when more official information might arrive.

There has been no confirmation from Rockstar Games or Take-Two, and no other major outlet has independently corroborated the report yet.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
