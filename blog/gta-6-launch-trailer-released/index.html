New GTA 6 Screenshot and Artwork Details

RockstarINTEL announced a brand new GTA 6 screenshot showing a floatplane from Brian Heder’s Boat Works & Marina soaring above the Grassrivers at sunset, captured in 4K resolution.

According to RockstarINTEL, the accompanying artwork, titled “Jason and Lucia: Lock and Load,” features the two protagonists standing together in Vice City, with both logo and logoless variants available for various aspect ratios.

Launch Trailer Context and Release Timeline

According to RockstarINTEL, the launch trailer, already released on Rockstar’s social channels as of 2 pm BST on 15 October 2026, confirms the November 19 2026 global launch date and introduces the main characters.

While the trailer provides visual confirmation, details about the game’s ending, new sweat system mechanics, and additional content remain undisclosed, with RockstarINTEL noting that further information will be revealed in upcoming updates.
