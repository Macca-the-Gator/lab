Event Finale Dates Leaked

According to RockstarINTEL, the upcoming GTA Online Event Week has been labeled as the 'Event Finale' and will run from September 24th to September 30th. The report, published on Wednesday, September 23rd, gives players a heads-up on the timing but does not detail what bonuses, discounts, or new content will be included.

What Remains Unknown

RockstarINTEL's post only confirms the event window. As of now, there is no official word from Rockstar Games on the specific activities, vehicle discounts, or double RP opportunities that typically accompany these weekly events. Players will need to wait for the official in-game announcement or further leaks to know what the finale will offer.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
