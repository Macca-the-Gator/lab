What Happened

A Reddit post picked up by Google News reports that Manni L. Perez has said she “doesn’t know” why her Instagram content reached 18 million views. The attention comes amid ongoing fan rumors that Perez is connected to Lucia, one of the lead characters in GTA 6.

Rumors vs. Confirmation

Per the Reddit report, Perez did not confirm any involvement with Rockstar Games or GTA 6. The viral Instagram views appear to have been fueled by speculation, but no official casting or character announcement has been made by Rockstar.

It remains unclear why the post gained so much traction or whether Perez has any role in the game. As with many GTA 6 rumors, fans should treat this as unverified until Rockstar or Perez herself provides a definitive statement.

What’s Next

For now, the only established fact is that Perez acknowledged the viral moment but offered no explanation. Whether this leads to more details about GTA 6’s cast or simply stays an internet mystery is still unknown.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
