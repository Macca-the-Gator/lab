Unconfirmed claims and evidence

Mission Overview

Spoiler warning: This secret GTA 6 mission on Hyde Island, reported by Gamespot, tasks players with surviving a hunter-driven scenario inspired by Hard Target and Saw. The mission triggers by landing on the island, getting darted, and waking in a shipping container with an explosive necklace. A captor's speech initiates the challenge, requiring players to reach a tent within 10 seconds to find a sidearm with only two rounds.

Context and analysis

Mission Walkthrough

After escaping the container, players must navigate Hyde Island while evading a hunter on a quad bike and his tracked dog Caesar. The hunter is heavily armored and equipped with a sniper rifle, making direct engagement risky. Strategy involves using trees for cover, prioritizing the dog due to its red-collared light, and finishing the hunter with shotgun rounds. Searching lit outposts provides better ammo and a shotgun. Successfully defeating the hunter yields the Island-Poacher FF-50 sniper rifle, ammo, and $15,000 cash from the boathouse, while failure results in cash loss and respawn near a safehouse.
