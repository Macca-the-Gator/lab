Six New Radio Stations and DJ Lineup

GameSpot reports that Rockstar Games has unveiled six radio stations for Grand Theft Auto 6, spanning modern Latin, renegade country, African music, heavy metal, pop, and regional hip-hop. Cocoteo FM hosts modern Latin music, Back Country covers renegade country, AfroBank FM features African tracks, The Chamber 106.6 offers heavy metal, Flash FM plays pop, and Dirty South Classics focuses on regional rap. Rockstar has reportedly recruited high-profile voices, with Bad Bunny and RaiNao starring as Cocoteo FM hosts and Lana Del Rey and Morgan Wallen headlining Back Country.

On-Foot Audio and Podcast Support

Eurogamer confirms a series first: both radio and podcasts will be available to listen to on foot through headphones and earbuds connected to smartphones, rather than solely in vehicles. Details on the specific podcast content remain unspecified, with Rockstar stating more information is coming soon. The announcement aligns with the game's scheduled November 19, 2026 release window.
