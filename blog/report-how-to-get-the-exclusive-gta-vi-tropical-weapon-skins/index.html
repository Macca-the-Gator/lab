Unconfirmed claims and evidence

What the GTA 6 Tropical Weapon Patterns Are

RockstarINTEL reports that GTA 6 includes two Vice City-inspired tropical weapon patterns that cannot be earned simply by playing the game. The outlet describes both designs as account-linked cosmetics rather than mission rewards or normal progression unlocks.

According to RockstarINTEL, the patterns show up as a Cosmetics / Wraps option inside the weapon modify menu at Ammu-Nation, and the outlet describes applying them as a very simple process once the account connection is complete.

Context and analysis

How to Unlock and Equip the Patterns

RockstarINTEL's steps start on the Rockstar Games website: sign in, then open Account > Manage Account > Connections and link the PlayStation or Xbox account that owns your copy of GTA 6. The outlet says you must reboot the game afterwards.

With the accounts connected, RockstarINTEL says to visit any Ammu-Nation store in Leonida and check the Cosmetics / Wraps section of the modify menu, where both Tropical Patterns will be listed among the available wraps.

Additional context

Weapon Limits and What Rockstar Hasn't Specified

RockstarINTEL cites Rockstar saying the pattern works on "most" weapons, not all of them. The outlet adds that no list of incompatible weapons has been released, so which guns are excluded remains unclear and players may need to check individually.

The same report notes GTA+ members have access to other exclusive GTA 6 content, which RockstarINTEL covers in a separate roundup. The report does not state an expiry date for the patterns or confirm whether the requirement changes on PC.
